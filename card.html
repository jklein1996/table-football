Open the game

Once a game is running, your Admin tab shows a QR with the game code built in. Show that one so nobody has to type the code.

Kickoff checklist (Joe)

  1. Open the game and tap Start a new game. Write the 4-letter code on a sticky note.
  2. Join a team yourself on the Teams tab. Pick quarters and minutes.
  3. Show the Admin-tab QR. Everyone joins Chiefs or Eagles.
  4. Laptop or TV: open the link, type the code, tap Join as TV.
  5. Tap Start the game, then Flip the coin.
  6. Keep your phone plugged in with the game open. The screen stays on by itself.

If something goes wrong

Your phone reloaded or closedOpen the game again and tap Resume game. Every play is saved.
Someone can't connectTurn their Wi-Fi off and use cell data, then rejoin with the code.
Nobody can connectOn your phone tap Play on this device and pass it around.
Your phone diesAny player's phone shows Take over hosting after 20 seconds. It continues from the last play with a new code.
Wrong tap or bad rollAdmin tab → Undo last play.
Screen says "Something hiccuped"Tap Reload. The game comes back where it was.

Real dice (optional)

Five dice in five colors. Each face reads as the number below. The app can roll for you instead.

Offense = black + white + blue (10–39). Defense = red + green (1–5).

Who wins the box

  • Check in order: flag first, then offense F.
  • Then a special defense box wins: (TD), ( ) number, QT, black, INT.
  • Then the offense box: B, QT, INT, black (incomplete).
  • Two plain numbers: add them.
  • Flag: the app re-rolls the play; the fouled team takes the better result.
  • QT → roll the QT column. B → roll the Breakaway column. INT → defense rolls its return. F → fumbling team rolls: 10–28 keeps it.

Clock

  • Time runs in 10-second boxes: each snap 10s; if the clock is running the huddle adds Normal 30s, Hurry-up 10s, Run the clock 40s. Quarters are 15 minutes.
  • Stops on: incomplete, out of bounds (*), flag, turnover, score, kick, timeout, spike.
  • Two-minute warning in the 2nd and 4th quarters. 3 timeouts per half.
  • Sideline call: get out of bounds (clock stops) or stay in bounds (clock runs); each can cost up to 5 yards.
  • A quarter can't end on a defensive penalty: one untimed down.
  • Tied at the end: overtime with NFL playoff rules. Coin toss, then both teams get the ball at least once, even if the first team scores a touchdown. Still tied after that: next score wins. 3 timeouts each.
